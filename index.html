Key Learnings

From my Personality Development (PESE 300) Course

UNIT 1: SWOC Analysis

This unit taught me the power of self-reflection. By analyzing my Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, and Challenges (SWOC), I created a clear roadmap for personal and academic growth. Understanding my own profile is the first step to improving it.

UNIT 2: Self Introduction

I learned to craft a clear and confident self-introduction. This went beyond just my name; it was about presenting my skills and aspirations effectively, making a strong and positive first impression (like an "elevator pitch").

UNIT 3: Presentation Skills

This unit was all about effective storytelling. I learned how to structure a presentation, design visually appealing slides, and engage an audience. We practiced how to convey complex ideas clearly and persuasively.

UNIT 4: Public Speaking

Focused on building confidence and overcoming stage fright. Through practical exercises like JAM sessions and extempore speeches, I learned to manage anxiety, think on my feet, and deliver my message with clarity.

UNIT 5: Art of Conversation

This final unit tied everything together. It taught me that communication is more than just talking—it's about active listening, understanding non-verbal cues (body language), and building genuine rapport.
